MINI RACER — version history
============================

Play: double-click MiniRacer.html (always the latest version). Press F for fullscreen, H for controls.
Old versions live in the "versions" folder. To go back to one, open it directly,
or copy it over MiniRacer.html.

v27 — 2026-09-25 — versions\v27_2026-09-25_new-ui.html
--------------------------------------------------------
UI overhaul:
- Fills the window at any size, sharp on any screen (wide screens show more
  track). F = fullscreen. The help line above the game is gone.
- New menu: settings grouped (Race / Conditions / AI), a track card (map,
  road/street, direction, length, corners, lap records), a big START button.
  Everything works with the mouse too (click rows or their arrows).
- H: controls overlay, from anywhere.
- Race HUD: timing tower is compact (leader + the cars round you); Tab for
  the full order; click a row to follow that car when watching. One stack of
  notices (flags, safety car, final lap, pit messages, lap times) instead of
  messages all over the screen.
- Live lap delta vs your best lap (green = up, red = down), gaps to the cars
  ahead/behind, and a ▲/▼ flash when you gain or lose a place.
- New speedo: rev lights, gear, speed, tyre ring, damage, surface.
- Minimap: 3 sizes (N, or click it), position numbers on the leaders and the
  cars around you.
- F1-style start: five red lights come on one by one, then GO!
- Pause menu (P): resume, restart, sound, minimap, timing tower, fullscreen,
  controls, quit. Keyboard or mouse.
- Results: podium with the top 3 cars, your grid-to-finish (places gained),
  times, best lap, stops, wall hits, and a lap-by-lap position chart; buttons
  for race again / replay / menu. Spectate results get the podium too.
- Spectate: clickable camera buttons, click any car to follow it, and a
  driver card (position, gap, best lap, stops, tyres, aggression, mistakes).
- One consistent look for every panel, button and notice.

v26 — 2026-09-25 — versions\v26_2026-09-25_sc-unlap-fix-closer-pace.html
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Safety car fix: lapped cars no longer get stuck on it. The safety car now
  waits for the front of the queue behind it (it used to crawl at ~88 km/h
  waiting for the leader, who could be stuck behind other cars after
  pitting). Only cars that really are a lap or more down are waved past, and
  the safety car moves over to the right while they go by on the left.
  Green flag when the front of the queue reaches the line.
- Closer pace: the slowest AI driver is now at most 5% off the fastest, however
  many cars (Race mode used to drop 1.2% per rival: the last of 59 rivals was
  at 30% pace and got lapped every lap). Spectate's spread cut from 10% to 5%.
- Menu: 40+ AI cars turns the number red and shows a flashing
  "expect total chaos" warning.

v25 — 2026-09-25 — versions\v25_2026-09-25_jeddah-60-cars-free-cam.html
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- New track: Jeddah Corniche, a fast, flowing, walled street circuit
  (anticlockwise): start straight, T1-T2 chicane, fast esses, a big banked
  left, flat-out kinks back along the Red Sea, tight final hairpin. Marina in
  the middle, modern towers with helipads, palms, a giant flagpole plaza.
  Championships now run 9 rounds.
- Menu: AI aggression (Low / Normal / High) and AI mistakes (Off / Low /
  Normal / High). Engine failures happen whatever the mistakes setting.
- Up to 60 cars (59 rivals + you, or 60 in Spectate), with 40 new drivers.
  Long fields: the live standings show the top 8 plus a window around the
  car you're watching; qualifying/results split into columns; the
  championship screen shows the points finishers / top 20 plus you.
- Retiring no longer leaves you staring at your wreck: the spectator cameras
  take over (TV director, follow, whole track, free) for the rest of the race.
- Spectate: new Free camera (C to cycle, or just use the mouse). Arrows or
  drag to pan, + / - or the mouse wheel to zoom; it can't leave the map. The
  minimap shows what you're looking at.
- Spectate: the TV director no longer cuts to every pit stop.
- Safety car: once the wreck is cleared, lapped cars (and any car between
  the safety car and the leader) are waved past to unlap themselves: the
  queue keeps right, they pass on the left. The safety car only comes in
  when they're all through.

v24 — 2026-09-25 — versions\v24_2026-09-25_aggressive-ai.html
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Aggressive AI: every driver gets an aggression level (some are much more
  aggressive than others). They follow closer and will bump the car ahead,
  dive up the inside into corners braking later, move across to defend
  against a quicker car behind, and squeeze cars alongside. This includes you.
- AI mistakes, more likely for error-prone drivers, in the wet, on worn
  tyres, with a damaged car or while attacking: running wide, locking up and
  going straight on, spinning, and (rarely) losing it at speed and crashing
  into the barriers, which usually wrecks the car.
- Engine failures: now and then a car loses power, smokes, pulls off the
  track and retires (yellow flags; safety car only if it stops on the track).
- Result: roughly 1 in 3 races (Normal damage) has a retirement, about half
  of races on High. Contact, moves and running wide are commented on, spaced
  out so the ticker isn't flooded; the TV director cuts to incidents.
- Fix: a car cruising round after the flag could set a 0.00 "fastest lap".

v23 — 2026-09-25 — versions\v23_2026-09-25_slipstream-wrecks-safety-car.html
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Fairer tyre wear: your car's handling drifts sideways far more than the
  AI's through a normal corner, and all of it used to count as wear (~50%
  more per lap than the AI). Now a clean lap costs about the same as the AI's;
  big slides, kerbs and running wide still cost extra.
- Slipstream: tuck in within ~170px behind another car on the straight for
  less drag and up to ~7% more top speed (more the closer you are). The AI
  uses it too. Wind streaks show it; SLIPSTREAM lights up on your speedo.
- Wrecks: a car that reaches 100% damage stops dead, catches fire and is out
  of the race (DNF, no points). Marshals clear it after ~12s.
- Safety car: a wreck brings it out. Everyone lines up single file behind
  it with no overtaking (your top speed is capped behind the car in front),
  no slipstream and no blue flags. It stays out at least a lap until the
  wreck is cleared and the field has bunched up, then pits; green flag when
  the leader reaches the line.
- Menu: Damage is now Off / Normal / High (High = 2.5x damage), and a new
  "Your damage" On/Off switch (Race) protects your car only.

v21 — 2026-09-25 — versions\v21_2026-09-25_grandstands-and-fixes.html
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Grandstands on every circuit (3-8 each): the main stand on the start
  straight, more on the outside of the biggest corners and along straights.
  Scenery is built around them. Crowds are animated: they fidget, jump about
  when cars go past, and a Mexican wave runs along each stand now and then.
- 6ix7seven: much less runoff.
- AI pit rule: no AI driver pits with 40% or more tyre left (tyre stops and
  damage repairs, when tyre wear is on). Strategies are planned around it.
- Race starts: the AI hold their grid columns while the pack spreads out,
  and when passing they go round on the side with more room instead of
  always heading for the middle. Much less bunching off the line.
- Speedometer reading eased so it no longer flickers.

v18 — 2026-09-25 — versions\v18_2026-09-25_ghost-codes.html
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Ghost codes: race your friends' best laps.
  Share: in Time Trial press G. Your best-ever time-trial lap on that track
  is copied as a code (asks your name the first time). Send it to a friend.
  Load: on the menu press G and paste a code. The game switches to that
  track in Time Trial; the friend's ghost is a see-through blue car with
  their name over it. Each lap shows how far ahead/behind their time you
  were, with confetti when you first beat it.
  Your best lap per track is saved automatically whenever you set one.

v17 — 2026-09-25 — versions\v17_2026-09-25_changing-weather.html
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Changing weather (Weather: Random, races only). The start weather is rolled
  as before, then each race secretly rolls 0-3 changes at random points:
  rain can arrive, stop, or pass over as a shower. Nothing is announced until
  it happens. Rain builds over a few seconds; the track soaks over ~15s and
  takes ~40s to dry once it stops, so grip changes gradually. Night races can
  now get rain too. HUD shows Damp / Wet while the track is wet.
- Rain grip, acceleration, AI pace and spray now scale with how wet the
  track is (fixed Rain weather behaves the same as before).

v15 — 2026-09-25 — versions\v15_2026-09-25_pit-strategy.html
--------------------------------------------------------------
- AI pit strategy: each driver picks its own plan at the start (the fewest
  stops its tyres allow, or about 1 in 3 go for an extra stop on fresher
  tyres) and splits its stints unevenly, so some stop early and some go long.
  Drivers are also harder or gentler on their tyres (+/-15% wear). Stops are
  now spread over many laps instead of the whole field pitting together.
- No car-to-car collisions in the pit lane: cars pass through each other once
  they're past the entry ramp (including in their boxes), so no more pile-ups.

v8 — 2026-09-25 — versions\v8_2026-09-25_damage.html
-----------------------------------------------------
- Car damage (races only): wall hits above ~90 km/h and heavy car-to-car
  contact damage the car. Damage lowers top speed (up to -18%) and steering
  (up to -25%); badly damaged cars (30%+) trail smoke. Pit stops repair it,
  and you can now pit for repairs even with tyre wear off. AI boxes when
  badly damaged. DMG % shown in your speed panel; commentary announces it.
